## Honest entry draft

**Problem:** Small merchants need to connect an official recall to order identifiers and distinguish manufacturer instructions from their own refund authority. Merchant demand remains unvalidated.

**What it does:** Official source → evidence-backed extraction → reviewed exact scope → deterministic triage → independent operator approval → durable courtesy-refund attempt and receipt.

**PayPal:** Direct REST sandbox order creation, buyer approval, server capture, owned-payment preflight, a single reserved refund write, and exact GET reconciliation are implemented and exercised. The owner approved a USD 49.00 purchase and courtesy refund on November 8, 2026. PayPal GET verified the completed refund; a returned sandbox-link hostname mismatch was fixed and the original result recovered without another POST. Repeated approval execution returned the same durable attempt. The saved proof explicitly records GET recovery and the untagged local build.
