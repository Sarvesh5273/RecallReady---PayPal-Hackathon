## Source evidence and visual assets

Official recall evidence comes from the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC), recall 26-090, displayed November 13, 2025. The preserved source snapshot retains its canonical URL and source attribution. Official-source content is not original RecallReady code and is not relicensed under MIT.

Belkin and PayPal names identify the recalled products and the sandbox payment integration. No manufacturer partnership or endorsement is claimed. The project’s original interface artwork is implemented in HTML/CSS; icons are provided by Lucide under ISC. The codebase does not bundle a soundtrack or externally generated stock images.
